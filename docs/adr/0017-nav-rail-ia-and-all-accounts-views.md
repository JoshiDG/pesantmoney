# Adopt Monarch's nav-rail structure: accounts move to their own screen, and Transactions/Recurring/Investments become all-accounts views

ADR-0007 already commits to matching Monarch's information architecture rather than its visual design. This ADR resolves a concrete question that decision left open: PesantMoney's sidebar today conflates navigation with the account list itself (accounts are rows inline in the nav, and selecting one jumps straight into that account's own transaction ledger). Monarch's actual structure is different in a way that turns out not to be cosmetic: the sidebar is a pure nav rail, "Accounts" is its own screen, and Transactions/Recurring/Investments are each a single all-accounts view (with an account filter), not a per-account destination.

Adopting that structure means the sidebar becomes a flat icon rail (Dashboard, Accounts, Transactions, Reports, Budget, Recurring, Goals, Investments, Settings); the account list moves to a new dedicated Accounts screen; and clicking an account there opens Transactions pre-filtered to it rather than a separate per-account ledger page. Categories, Rules, and Merchants — which have no Monarch top-level nav equivalent — move from top-level rail items into tabs inside Settings.

The consequential part is backend, not just layout: `list_transactions`, and the per-account `list_for_account` queries backing Recurring Items and Holdings, all require a mandatory `account_id` today. Matching Monarch's nav semantics requires new all-accounts query variants for all three, plus new aggregate queries for a Reports screen (cross-account cash flow, spending-by-category, income-by-category) that don't exist in any form yet. This is real, hard-to-reverse backend surface, not a pure frontend reshuffle — worth recording so a future contributor understands why these all-accounts queries exist alongside the original per-account ones, rather than assuming the per-account queries were simply an oversight to be deleted.

Two related scope decisions from the same discussion, kept here rather than as separate ADRs since they're smaller and easily revisited:

- Monarch's own IA has redundancy — a standalone "Cash Flow" nav item shows near-identical content to the "Cash Flow" tab inside its tabbed "Reports" module. PesantMoney unifies these into a single Reports screen (Cash Flow/Spending/Income tabs) rather than shipping two screens with duplicate content.
- Monarch's "Forecasting" nav item is a paywalled marketing upsell (a "Plus" retirement-planning teaser), not a real feature with an underlying data model — there's nothing concrete to replicate structurally, and it would require net-worth/scenario projection work with no relation to anything Monarch actually ships. It's out of scope for this pass; a future Forecasting feature, if pursued, needs its own scoping ADR the way Goal Pace (ADR-0015) and Payoff Projection (ADR-0016) got theirs.

Once Recurring and Investments exist as top-level all-accounts screens, the per-account ledger's own embedded Holdings/Recurring tabs (in the existing per-account transaction view) are removed — keeping them would mean two different places to manage the same data.
